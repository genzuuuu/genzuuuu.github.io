Max Planck Institute for Chemical Physics of Solids

Ph.D. Student

In my understanding, the most important role of DFT is to simplify the problem step by step to our acceptable amount of computation in order to study the electronic structure. I have always thought that approximations are the most beautiful part of physics. Although DFT solutions are accurate, they are on a very small scale. Some traditional molecular dynamics methods are on a larger scale but have accuracy issues. I think the trade-off between accuracy and scale is also a challenge that persists throughout computational physics. Research on using deep learning methods to optimize this trade-off has aroused my great interest. Currently, this type of research is mostly limited to the Hamiltonian, so I would like to spend a few years investigating the neural network wave function to pursue a PhD degree here.
